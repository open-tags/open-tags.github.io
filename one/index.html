before you buy.

Is this a finished consumer tracker?

No. opentag one is small-batch development hardware for ranging and localization experiments.

What do I need for TDOA?

Use multiple tags as anchors plus one mobile tag. The 5-tag kit is the intended starting point.

Can I flash it from the browser?

Yes. The firmware page supports flashing and ranging workflows through browser tooling.

Is the design open?

Yes. Hardware files, firmware, and CAD are published through the OpenTags project.
