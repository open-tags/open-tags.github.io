Brand guidelines

A clear system for serious hardware.

opentags should feel precise, direct, and easy to work with. The identity is intentionally restrained so the product and its evidence do the talking.

Principles

Every decision starts here.

01

Simple by default.

Remove choices, decoration, and terminology until only what helps someone understand or use the product remains.

02

Transparent by design.

Publish interfaces, dimensions, source, limitations, and test results. Specific evidence is more useful than broad claims.

Naming

One name. One product.

Companyopentags

Lowercase, plural, and one word. Use this for the company and manufacturer.

Productopentag one

Lowercase. Use “Distance Kit” and “Location Kit” only when distinguishing packages.

Domain + GitHubopen-tags

The hyphen belongs in URLs and account names, never in normal brand copy.

Voice

Say exactly what it does.

Know where your robots are.

Open UWB hardware for distance and indoor positioning.

Lead with the outcome.

Distance between two points. Position inside a space.

Use concrete language.

Say USB-C, ROS 2, CAN FD, 6–24 V, and 186 × 132 mm when those details matter.

Earn industrial credibility.

Show specifications, protocols, CAD, validation, and limitations. Avoid empty claims like “revolutionary.”

Typography

Editorial for impact. Sans for clarity.

Editorial New
Distance or location.

Use only for major product headlines and white titles placed over product photography.

System sans
Built to integrate. Ready for your robot.

Use for navigation, section headings, body copy, specifications, labels, buttons, documentation, and code.

No monospace type. Technical content should remain readable and consistent with the rest of the system.

Color

Black, white, and neutral gray.

Ink#111111
Paper#FFFFFF
Muted#666666
Soft#F7F7F7
Line#DDDDDD

There is no brand accent color. Links, selected states, buttons, diagrams, and focus treatments stay monochrome. Functional warning colors are reserved for actual system status.

Four-pixel corners.

Controls and media use restrained corners, never soft pill-shaped decoration.

Thin rules.

Use neutral one-pixel borders to organize information instead of boxed-in card effects.

Space is structure.

Use generous vertical spacing and a 1120 px maximum content width. Avoid dense dashboards on marketing pages.

Photography

Show the hardware clearly.

Use real product imagery.Show the device, connections, enclosure, and application context without decorative renders.

Keep titles short.Use “opentag one,” “Distance,” or “Location” in white Editorial New over the image.

Protect legibility.Use a subtle black lower gradient. Do not tint imagery with a brand color.
